Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my bridal appointment?

We recommend booking your bridal consultation 9-12 months before your wedding date to allow adequate time for design, creation, and fittings.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we offer complimentary worldwide shipping on all orders. All items are fully insured and require signature upon delivery.

What is your bespoke design process timeline?

Bespoke haute couture pieces typically require 3-6 months from initial consultation to final delivery, depending on the complexity of the design.

Do you offer virtual styling services?

Yes, we offer virtual consultations via video call. Our stylists can show you pieces from our collections and provide personalized recommendations.
