The Maison Élégance Story

Founded in 1947 in the heart of Paris, Maison Élégance was born from a vision to create garments that transcend time and trend. Our founder, Madame Éloise Laurent, believed that true elegance comes from the perfect marriage of exceptional craftsmanship and timeless design.

From our humble beginnings as a small atelier on Avenue Montaigne, we have grown into a globally recognized fashion house while maintaining our commitment to the artisanal techniques that have defined us from the start.

Today, three generations later, we continue to honor Madame Laurent's legacy, creating pieces that celebrate the art of haute couture and the beauty of individual expression.
